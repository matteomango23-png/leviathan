# Prompt delle tre bestie iniziali (e delle loro evoluzioni)

Tre creature preistoriche, uniche, di tre tipi diversi; ognuna ha 3 stadi (si evolve ai livelli 16 e 36).
Salva le immagini dove vuoi (anche sul Desktop): le riconosco e le sistemo io. Se riesci, mettile in una cartella per creatura.

**Come lavorare in Gemini:** una chat per ogni linea (Zanna, Guscio, Scintilla). Nella stessa chat, nell'ordine:
1. la card dello stadio 1;
2. le 4 viste dello stadio 1 (prompt A-D qui sotto);
3. la card dello stadio 2 (*"the same creature grown up"*) e le sue 4 viste;
4. lo stesso per lo stadio 3.

Così lo stile e i colori restano uguali.

**Priorità:** se hai poco tempo, prima le **card dei 3 cuccioli** (servono per la scelta iniziale), poi le viste **davanti e da dietro** dei cuccioli (servono in battaglia). Il resto dopo.

---

## Prompt comuni

**Card** (sostituisci `[CREATURE]` e `[ACCENT]`):

```
Dark fantasy creature card illustration, realistic digital painting, [CREATURE], full body in a dynamic three-quarter side view, swimming in the deep ocean, pitch-black water fading to deep teal, dramatic rim light from a single diver's lamp, drifting marine snow particles, [ACCENT], highly detailed skin texture, ominous and majestic mood, centered composition with empty dark space around, the whole body inside the picture, no text, no border, no watermark, vertical 4:5
```

**Card degli stadi 2 e 3** (nella stessa chat):

```
The same creature, grown up: [CREATURE]. Same painting style, same colors and markings, clearly the same lineage but bigger and more powerful. Same composition rules as the previous card, vertical 4:5, no text.
```

Poi, sempre nella stessa chat, le 4 viste. Valgono per ogni stadio:

| | File | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| A | profilo | `Same creature, same style and colors. A perfectly flat side profile view, facing RIGHT, body completely horizontal and straight, mouth closed, the whole body including the tail fully inside the picture with a wide empty margin all around, isolated on a pure flat black background, no floor, no shadow, no haze, no particles, no light rays, no text.` |
| B | profilo a bocca aperta | `Edit this image: same exact pose, size and position, but with the jaws wide open showing the teeth.` |
| C | davanti (la bestia avversaria) | `Same creature, same style and colors. Three-quarter front view, coming toward the viewer and turned slightly to the LEFT, the whole body including the tail fully inside the picture with a wide empty margin all around, nothing touches the edges, isolated on a pure flat black background, no floor, no shadow, no haze, no particles, no light rays, no text. Square image.` |
| D | da dietro (la tua bestia) | `Same creature, same style and colors. Three-quarter rear view from slightly above, swimming away from the viewer toward the upper RIGHT, back clearly visible, head slightly turned to the right, the whole body fully inside the picture with a wide empty margin all around, nothing touches the edges, isolated on a pure flat black background, no floor, no shadow, no haze, no particles, no light rays, no text. Square image.` |
