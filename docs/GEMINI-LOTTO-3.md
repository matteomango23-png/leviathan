## Come si chiede

Una chat nuova per ogni forma, con allegata la card della bestia base (`asset animali ai/<bestia>/<id>_card.jpg`).

1. **Card:**
   ```

   ```
2. **Profilo:**
   ```
   The same evolved creature, exactly the same design and colors. Perfectly flat side view facing RIGHT, body straight and horizontal, the whole body and tail fully inside the picture with a wide empty margin, mouth closed. Isolated on a flat pure green background (#00FF00), no shadow, no particles, no light rays, no text. Wide 2:1 image.
   ```
3. **Davanti:**
   ```
   The same evolved creature, same design. Three-quarter FRONT view, head toward the viewer turned slightly to the LEFT, the whole body fully inside the picture with a wide empty margin, nothing touches the edges. Flat pure green background (#00FF00), no floor, no shadow, no particles, no text. Square image.
   ```
4. **Dietro:**
   ```
   Start from the side profile above. The same creature seen from BEHIND at 45 degrees: the camera is behind it and a little to its left, we see its back, the back of its head and its tail closest to us, swimming away into the picture toward the upper right. ONE body, ONE head, ONE tail, same design. The whole body fully inside the picture with a wide empty margin. Flat pure green background (#00FF00), no floor, no shadow, no particles, no text. Square image.
   ```

**Accento per tipo:**
- predatore: `blood-red rim light`
- abissale: `cyan bioluminescent glow`
- glaciale: `pale icy-blue light and floating ice shards`
- tempesta: `violet and electric-yellow lightning arcs`
- corazzato: `bronze and amber highlights on armor plates`
